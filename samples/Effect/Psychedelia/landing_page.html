Psychedelia for After Effects

Features

30+ Dynamic Parameters

Take complete creative control. Tweak over 30 dynamic parameters—including Fractal Geometry, Color Separation, Edge Halo, and Palette Blend—to customize every detail of your effect.

Warping, Breathing & Melting

Bring your visuals to life with dynamic motion controls. Apply complex fluid warp, breathing animations, and organic melt dynamics to your patterns.

Liquid Light & Tracers

Emulate vintage liquid oil projector layouts and feedback loop tracers with built-in hue shifting, spectrum drift, and alpha stencil masks for text overlays.
